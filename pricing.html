ONBOARDING & DUE DILIGENCE

How Program & Compute Evaluation Works

We follow a structured three-step qualification process to ensure technical alignment before allocating pilot or enterprise compute resources.

STEP 01 // INITIAL INQUIRY

Direct Founder Correspondence

Contact founder@vidsynth.app outlining your studio, research objectives, target video formats, and desired conditioning passes.

STEP 02 // TECHNICAL SCOPING

Pipeline & Security Review

We evaluate resolution targets, frame counts, color-space requirements (Rec.709 / ACEScg), and data retention expectations against available compute capacity.

STEP 03 // CONFIRMATION

Written Pilot or Compute Agreement

Approved partners receive confirmed terms specifying sponsored grant scope or custom enterprise pricing prior to any media transfer or billing.
