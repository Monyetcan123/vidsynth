COMMERCIAL FAQ

Billing & Licensing Questions

Why are dollar prices not listed for Pro VFX and Enterprise?

VidSynth is onboarding studios in structured cohorts. Rather than publishing speculative pricing or unverified SLA guarantees, we provide free Sandbox evaluation and tailor commercial agreements directly with onboarded teams.

Do unused Sandbox evaluation credits expire?

Sandbox evaluation credits do not expire during technical preview, and can be reset inside the Studio Dashboard for continued workflow testing.

Who owns the intellectual property of generated outputs?

Under our Terms of Service, customers retain full ownership of their uploaded source plates and synthesized outputs. Customer plates are never used to train foundation models without explicit written opt-in.
